Help & Support

How do I get a match?

Like someone from Today’s Picks on Home. When they like you back, you match and a chat opens under Chat.

How do I plan a date?

Open the Date tab, choose a match, pick a day, time and place, and send the request. Your match can accept or reject it.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap their card on Home and choose Report or Block, or use the ⋮ menu inside a chat.

How do I change my photo or details?

Tap your name at the top of this page for the photo, or open Personal Information to edit your details.
